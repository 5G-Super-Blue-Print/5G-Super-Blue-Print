# 5G Super Blueprints Maintainers, Contributors, and Committers
# The 5G SBP is a unique project that does not depend on code contributions to be successful. As such, Maintainer, Contributor, and Committer share the same definition. Maintainer, Contributor, and Committer are defined as someone who contributes to a 5G SBP project, contributes code, documentation, wiki content, or provides technical or strategic guidance to the community.
